Important Instructions for Applicants
  • Download the relevant challan form, fill in all required details clearly, and deposit the prescribed fee at the designated bank branch.
  • Attach the original paid bank challan/deposit slip with the registration or evaluation application, as applicable.
  • Applications submitted without a valid paid challan will not be processed.
  • Ensure the CNIC/Organization details on the challan match the details provided in the application form.
  • For queries regarding fee amounts or bank details, contact the NSDRA office at info@nsdra.gov.pk or +92-51-9265453.
